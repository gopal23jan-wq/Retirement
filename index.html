📖 How to Use This Calculator
▼

What This Tool Does

This calculator projects how long your retirement savings will last based on your income, investments, and spending — specific to Canada. It updates in real time as you change any value. No login, no data stored anywhere.

Step-by-Step Guide

1 · Your Profile

Enter your name (used in the PDF report), age, province, and life expectancy. Life expectancy is a planning horizon — use 90–95 to be conservative. The inflation and investment return sliders have sensible defaults (2.5% / 5%), but adjust them to match your own portfolio mix.

2 · Guaranteed Income

Enter your monthly CPP, OAS, and any workplace pension. Use the CPP Start Age and OAS Start Age dropdowns to see how deferring affects your income. The calculator applies the correct government adjustment factors automatically. An OAS clawback warning appears if your income exceeds the 2024 threshold (~$91K).

3 · Investments & Savings

Enter your current balances for RRSP/RRIF, TFSA, and Non-Registered accounts. Add your home value if you plan to factor it into your estate — you can set an annual appreciation rate. A RRIF conversion reminder appears if you are under 71 with RRSP funds.

4 · Retirement Spending (3 Phases)

Unlike flat-spending models, this calculator uses three phases that reflect how spending actually changes in retirement:
  Go-Go (65–75) — active years, highest spending (travel, hobbies, dining)
  Slow-Go (75–85) — more home-based, moderate spending
  No-Go (85+) — lowest lifestyle spending, but add Long-Term Care if applicable
Enter your expected annual spend for each phase. Leave blank if unsure — start with your current annual expenses as a guide.

5 · Spouse / Partner

Toggle on to include your spouse's CPP, OAS, and pension. Their income is added to the household pool. You can also enable Pension Income Splitting (up to 50%) to reduce your combined tax burden.

6 · Dependent Adult Child

Toggle on if you provide ongoing financial support to an adult child. Enter the annual amount you contribute and any RDSP contributions you make. ODSP/disability benefits your child receives are shown for reference — they do not automatically reduce your cost (adjust your Annual Support figure to reflect any offset). The total cost is inflation-adjusted and flows through your retirement projection.

Reading the Results Bar

The sticky bar at the top shows three key numbers at all times:

  • 🟢 Money Lasts Until — the age at which your portfolio runs to zero, or "Age [X]+" if it lasts your full life expectancy
  • 🔵 Max Safe Go-Go Spend — the maximum annual spending in your active years that keeps your portfolio sustainable (based on guaranteed income + 3.5% of portfolio)
  • 🟡 Est. Estate at Life Exp. — the projected value of what you leave behind (portfolio + projected home value + other assets)

The Detailed Analysis Tabs

  • Income — your full income breakdown with estimated tax by province
  • Projection — a chart of your portfolio value over time, with phase shading and milestone values at ages 75, 85, and life expectancy
  • CPP Deferral — a table comparing total lifetime CPP at every start age, with break-even points
  • Scenarios — survival under Bear (2%), Caution (4%), Balanced (6%), and Growth (8%) returns, plus four stress-test buttons (market crash, high inflation, long life, spending cut)

Exporting Your Report

Click 📄 Export Report (PDF) at the top to open a print-ready summary in a new tab. Click the blue "Save as PDF" button on that page — on Mac, choose "Save as PDF" from the print dialog; on iPhone, use the share sheet to "Save to Files".

Important Notes

⚠️ This calculator is for personal planning and educational purposes only. It does not constitute financial, tax, or legal advice. Tax estimates are simplified and do not capture all credits, surtaxes, or personal circumstances.

Always consult a licensed financial planner or tax advisor before making major retirement decisions.
